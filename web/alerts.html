Severity classes
Class 01
Shortfall ahead

Forecast demand outruns usable stock inside 72 hours. The alert carries the nearest bank with spare units of the same component.

Class 02
Expiry risk

Units will cross shelf life before local demand can absorb them. Platelets trip this at 48 hours, whole blood at seven days.

Class 03
Surplus match

A district's excess lines up with another's forecast gap. Both banks get the same pairing, with the drive time attached.
