What is Hemzo?

A forecast of blood stock, district by district.

What it tells you

For each district and blood group: how much stock there is, how that compares with normal, and the chance of running short in the next three days.

Where it comes from

The government's public eRaktKosh stock register, read every few hours and kept as a record. Nothing private and nothing patient-level.

Who it is for

Blood-bank staff, hospital planners and health officers who decide where units should move. Anyone can read it. It supports a decision and does not replace one.
